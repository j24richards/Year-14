    Weekly Results▸

    Pick a week, then type each matchup's two teams and scores — or paste the Yahoo scoreboard text and press Preview. Nothing is published until you press Save Changes. A week with no filled-in games is removed.

    Season Grades▸

    Roster and Season grades show live on the Results page. After the last regular-season week, lock them in so they stay as the final grades, then press Save Changes.

    In-Season Points Per Game▸

    Optional. One player per line as Player Name, points per game (a tab between them works too). Paste as many rostered players as you can — the Trade Analyzer ranks each player against others at his position and blends that with the preseason ranking. Leave it empty to use preseason rankings only.

    Team Badges▸

    Optional: paste an emoji per team to use as its badge instead of the auto-generated initials.

    Keeper Selections▸

    Pick up to four kept players per team. The Rd field is the player's base draft round (where they were picked or kept last year) — it fills in automatically but can be corrected by hand. The ADP field is the player's Fantasy Football Calculator Half-PPR ADP (average draft position, lower = more valuable) — Half PPR is used since this league is half-PPR scoring. When two keepers land on the same round, the round shown on the Keepers page is auto-resolved using ADP: the more valuable player moves up a round (except at round 1, where the more valuable player keeps round 1 and the other moves up to round 2 instead). ADP values are tied to the season they're entered for — an amber-highlighted ADP field means that value is left over from a prior year and is being ignored until you re-enter and save it for this season.

    Keeper Rules Text▸

    Wording shown on the Rules page under "Keeper Rules." Edit any line below and Save Changes to publish it.

    The mock draft bots learn a manager's real draft-day tendencies from their own past drafts once there are two or more seasons on file for them.     2026 Team Rosters — Position & NFL Team▸

    Fill in each player's position and NFL team so they show up on the 2026 Teams page. Generate the rosters above first. Each team's badge shows when its roster last changed (click it to jump to that day in Roster History). Use the × to drop a player (waiver drop, bye trade-away, etc.) and “+ Add Player” to pick one up — this is also where you keep a roster current after waiver moves and trades, not just after the draft.

    Roster History▸

    Snapshots are grouped by day: each day shows the roster as of your last Save Changes that day, and lists which teams changed. The 2026 Teams page always shows the newest one — click a day to view an older snapshot (read-only). You can also click a team's “Updated” badge in 2026 Team Rosters to jump straight to the day it last changed.
