Keeper Selections▸

Pick up to four kept players per team. The Rd field is the player's base draft round (where they were picked or kept last year) — it fills in automatically but can be corrected by hand. The ADP field is the player's Fantasy Football Calculator Half-PPR ADP (average draft position, lower = more valuable) — Half PPR is used since this league is half-PPR scoring. When two keepers land on the same round, the round shown on the Keepers page is auto-resolved using ADP: the more valuable player moves up a round (except at round 1, where the more valuable player keeps round 1 and the other moves up to round 2 instead). ADP values are tied to the season they're entered for — an amber-highlighted ADP field means that value is left over from a prior year and is being ignored until you re-enter and save it for this season.
