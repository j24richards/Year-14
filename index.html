    Pick up to two kept players per team. Enter each player's base draft round (Rd) and Fantasy Football Calculator PPR ADP (lower = more valuable) — used to auto-resolve round conflicts when two keepers land on the same round. ADP values are tied to the season they're entered for — an amber-highlighted ADP field means that value is left over from a prior year and is being ignored until you re-enter and save it for this season.
